How We Work: Reliable Service, Built on Process

Great technology work isn't just skill — it's consistency. Every request tracked, every change documented, every client kept informed. Here's the operating standard behind DPTC, so you know exactly what to expect before you ever work with us.

Our Operating Standard

Six ways we keep the work reliable

Steady, dependable, and transparent — no dropped requests, no mystery, no "I'll get to it eventually."

01 · Intake

Every request is tracked

Nothing lives in a text thread or someone's memory. Every request is logged, prioritized, and owned — so it actually gets done and you can see where it stands.

02 · Documentation

Your setup, written down

We keep a clear record of your systems, access, and past work. Consistent results even when we're busy — and no starting from scratch every time.

03 · Response Times

Defined, not vague

You know how fast we respond before you sign — same-business-day on priority plans. We tell you the standard and we hold to it.

04 · Escalation

A real path when it matters

When something breaks or an issue is urgent, there's a defined severity path — not hoping the right person notices. Critical issues move first.

05 · Reporting

You see the value

On managed plans, a monthly summary of what was done, response times met, and where things stand. No black box — just proof of the work.

06 · Security & Governance

Handled the right way

Least-privilege access, clean offboarding, and change control on every account. Cybersecurity-trained discipline applied to your day-to-day operations.

Why It Matters
