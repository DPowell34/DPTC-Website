Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I have to change my shop's phone number?
No. Missed-Call Text-Back works alongside the number already on your door, your truck, and your Google listing. Callers dial the same number they always have — the automation only steps in when your front desk can't pick up.
What happens if my front desk does answer the call?
Nothing. The text-back only fires on calls that go unanswered, so customers you talk to live never receive an automated message. It only covers the calls you'd otherwise lose.
How fast is the text sent after a missed call?
Within about 60 seconds. The caller — even a "how much for brakes?" price-shopper — gets a text before they can dial the next shop on Google, and their reply lands in a shared inbox you can answer from your phone between jobs.
How long does it take to go live?
Straight answer: your text-back goes live the day your carrier registration (A2P 10DLC) clears — typically 1 to 3 weeks, and it's the same for everyone. We file it on day one and build everything else — your customer-list import, the reactivation cadence, the booking link — while it processes, so you're live the moment it's approved.
Is there a long-term contract?
No. Both plans are month-to-month — shops are burned on contracts, so we don't use them. Book a free Missed-Lead Audit and DPTC will walk you through pricing for your call volume and customer list.
