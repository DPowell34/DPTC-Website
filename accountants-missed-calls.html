How It Works

From Inquiry to Booked Consult in Four Steps

No new software for your clients to learn, nothing for your team to remember — the sequence runs on its own from the moment an inquiry lands.

1

An inquiry comes in

A new-client inquiry lands from your web form, phone, directory listing, or a referral. It enters the system with contact details logged.

2

The nurture begins

An automated, multi-week sequence goes out in your professional voice — a fast first reply, a helpful second touch, then steady, spaced follow-ups across the weeks the decision takes.

3

The prospect books a consult

Every touch carries a booking link tied to your real calendar. Interest turns into a scheduled consultation — and reminders cut the no-shows.

4

You sign the engagement

You do what you do best in the room. Every inquiry is captured, nurtured, and tracked by name, so nothing goes cold while you're heads-down.

The Math

One Client You Let Go Cold Pays for a Year

The decision to hire you takes weeks, sometimes months, because a stranger is choosing who to trust with their financial life.

Most firms follow up exactly once. You're not losing clients you can see — you're losing the ones who inquired, got one reply, and quietly signed with the firm that stayed in touch. A single recurring client kept from going cold covers the system for a year and more.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does this replace my staff or admin?
No. It handles the follow-up your team means to send but rarely has time for during tax season or month-end — a professional, multi-week nurture sequence on every new-client inquiry. Your staff still owns the relationships and the client work; the engine makes sure no inquiry goes cold while they're heads-down.
Is this compliant with CPA advertising rules?
Yes, within the rules, and we build to them. We follow up only with prospects who reached out to you first — not cold solicitation. Messages go out in your professional voice, accurate and non-exaggerated. Review requests invite an honest review at a natural moment and never touch confidential client information. If your state board has a specific rule, we build around it.
How fast does it start?
Email nurture — your primary channel — starts within days of onboarding, because email needs no carrier registration. SMS follow-up switches on once your carrier registration (A2P 10DLC) clears, typically 1–3 weeks. We file it day one and email carries the load while it processes.
Do you guarantee I'll sign new clients?
No. Whether a prospect signs depends on your own close conversation and pricing, which we don't control. We guarantee the part we control: inquiries captured, nurtured, and booked into consults — all visible by name on your Pipeline Scoreboard. That's what the 120-Day Pipeline Guarantee is measured on.
Is there a long-term contract?
No. Both plans are month-to-month, no annual lock. Start with a free Pipeline Leak Assessment and we'll walk you through pricing for your firm's inquiry volume.
