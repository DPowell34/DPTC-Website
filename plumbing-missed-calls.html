Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I have to change my business phone number?
No. Missed-Call Text-Back works alongside your existing business number. Callers dial the same number they always have — the automation only steps in when you can't answer.
What happens if I do answer the call?
Nothing. The text-back only fires on calls that go unanswered, so customers you talk to live never receive an automated message.
How fast is the text sent after a missed call?
Within seconds. The caller receives a text before they have time to dial the next company on their list, and their reply lands in a shared inbox you can answer from your phone.
How long does setup take?
Most plumbing companies are live within a few business days. DPTC handles the configuration, message templates, and testing — you just approve the wording.
Is there a long-term contract?
Plans are month-to-month. Book a free demo and DPTC will walk you through pricing for your call volume.
