Straight Answers

Questions pool owners actually ask

Does this replace my phone number or my office person?

Neither. It rides on your existing business number and only fires when a call goes unanswered. If someone picks up, nothing happens. It's a backstop, not a replacement.

How fast can it be live?

Typically inside two weeks from kickoff, including text-messaging compliance registration for your business number — legally required for business texting, and part of the setup rather than an extra.

What if my customers are older and don't text?

Then they leave a voicemail like they always have — you lose nothing. The system captures the callers who won't leave one, which is the group you're currently losing without knowing it.

Am I locked into a contract?

Setup fee plus month-to-month retainer. If the monthly recap doesn't justify the cost, you cancel. The reporting exists so that decision is made with your numbers, not our claims.
